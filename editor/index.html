Help & troubleshooting

To edit: pick a letter (dropdown or ‹ ›), read the original scan on the left (pinch to zoom, drag to move), fix the text on the right, click Save. When you're ready for it to go live, click Publish.

If Publish says it couldn't reach GitHub: you're probably offline. Your work is saved on your computer — just click Publish again when you're back online.

If the banner says the live site has newer changes: click Sync first, then Publish.

If anything looks broken (this page, or the website itself): double-click “Repair.command” in the love-always-gene folder. It checks and fixes things and tells you what to do. You can also rebuild the website files right now:

Every save is also copied into a .backups folder, so an edit is never lost. For a fresh Claude: everything is documented in CLAUDE.md and EDITING.md in the repo.
