# Identification Notes

and what is still open. The goal is "no more question marks" — this records the
progress toward it and the lookups that would close the rest.

## Confirmed and corrected this round

- **Joan** is **Joan Northcutt Lankford** (Northcutt was her maiden name);
  married Gene April 20, 1943. Her parents were Joe E. Northcutt (1887–1932)
  and Anna Green Wood (1898–1988).
- **Joan's mother** is now named: **Anna Green Wood** (was just "your mother").
- **Gene's siblings** (from the obituary), which clears most family question
  marks:
  - Brothers: **Thomas "Tom"**, **Carroll**, **Earl**, **James Edward "Jim"**
    (survived Gene; Danville, KY), and Lewis Zane and the infant twins William
    and Robert.
  - Sisters: **Martha Lankford Broaddus** and **Alberta Lankford Brady**.
  - So "Earl" was a **brother** (not a cousin), and "Alberta" was Gene's
    **sister** (not an ambiguous friend); "Jim" is **James Edward**.
