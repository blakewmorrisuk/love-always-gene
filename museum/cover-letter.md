Dear Mr. Tomlin,

In the summer of 2022 we corresponded about my grandfather's wartime letters,
and the museum kindly agreed to accept them into its collection once the family
had finished transcribing the correspondence and gathering the supporting
material you asked for. That work is now done, and it is my privilege to send it
to you.

Enclosed with the letters are the items you requested: a short biography of my
grandfather, Raymond Eugene "Gene" Lankford, and of my grandmother, Joan
Northcutt; a summary of his Navy service aboard the U.S.S. New Orleans
(CA-32); a "cast of characters" identifying the people named throughout the
letters and their relation to him; an overview of the collection and its
condition; and the completed donor log.

The full collection has also been transcribed and published online, with page
images of every letter, at blakewmorrisuk.github.io/love-always-gene, should
that be useful to your catalogers.

There is no more fitting home for these letters than the museum in the
New Orleans whose name my grandfather's ship carried through the Pacific. Thank
you, and your colleagues, for being their keepers.

With gratitude,

Blake William Morris
