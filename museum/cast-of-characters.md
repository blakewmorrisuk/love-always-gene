# Cast of Characters

The people mentioned throughout the wartime letters of Raymond Eugene "Gene" Lankford to Joan Northcutt, and their relation to the writer. Prepared for The National WWII Museum as donor-provided interpretive material.

_Identifications are drawn from the letters themselves and from family research. Entries marked (uncertain) rest on an ambiguous reading or an inferred relationship._

_51 people across 6 groupings._

## The Correspondents

_Gene and Joan._

**Joan Northcutt Lankford** — The recipient (Gene's wife)

The Stanford, Kentucky girl Gene courted by mail and married on April 20, 1943. Her maiden name was Northcutt; she lived on Route 1, Stanford, and is the recipient of nearly all the letters.

Note: Maiden name Northcutt. Daughter of Joe E. Northcutt (1887-1932) and Anna Green Wood (1898-1988); married Gene 20 Apr 1943. Joan's family confirmed via WikiTree (Northcutt-199).

**Raymond Eugene "Gene" Lankford** — The writer

The young sailor who wrote essentially all of these letters; born 1921 in Stanford, Lincoln County, Kentucky. He served aboard the U.S.S. New Orleans (CA-32) in the F Division, courting Joan Northcutt by mail throughout the war.

Note: Son of William Porter Lankford and Marguerite Lynn Lankford, of Stanford, Lincoln County, Kentucky.

## Navy Shipmates & Officers

_U.S.S. New Orleans (CA-32), F Division, and beyond._

Don Chapin's wife, whom Gene counted as a friend. A letter Gene sent her upset her, prompting Don's letter (L101). Don worried about her while at sea.

_Appears in 3 letters: L71, L88, L101_

Note: 'the wife he left in Seattle' (L71) inferred to be Betty. In L101 Don calls her only "my wife" (alias added so the jump highlights). L102's "Betty" is the Mitsubishi bomber Jack shot down, not her; deliberately not referenced.

_Appears in 6 letters: L62, L71, L88, L94, L101, L102_

**Jack "Johnny" Parkhurst** — Gene's Navy shipmate and closest friend (U.S.S. New Orleans, F Division, radar operator)

Gene's closest Navy friend aboard the U.S.S. New Orleans, a radar operator in the F Division who worked in the office. The two were constant companions on liberty, requested transfers together, and Jack (called 'Johnny' by his wife) married Mary Louise Pendleton on December 25 and was later transferred off the ship.

_Appears in 19 letters: L49, L51, L56, L58, L59, L61, L62, L64, L67, L68, L69, L71, L76, L80, L94, L100, L101, L102, L107_

Note: 'Johnny' confirmed as Jack's nickname by his wife's letter (L102). Married Mary Louise Pendleton on December 25, 1942 (L100 postscript). By late 1944 (L102): radar operator PO3c aboard U.S.S. Massachusetts ("the best operator on the ship," per Officer Riley), credited with downing a "Betty" bomber.

**Mary Louise Parkhurst** — Wife of Gene's shipmate Jack "Johnny" Parkhurst

Jack Parkhurst's bride (nee Mary Louise Pendleton), whom Jack married on December 25. She writes Gene a friendly letter (L102), noting Gene's picture appears in Johnny's Hawaii album and that he was the best radar operator on the ship.
