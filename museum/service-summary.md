# Service Summary

Prepared for The National WWII Museum in response to its question: "Where and
when did your grandfather serve, in what unit, and in what capacity?"

## Raymond Eugene "Gene" Lankford

- **Branch:** United States Navy
- **Enlisted:** Spring 1940, at age 18, from Stanford, Lincoln County, Kentucky
- **Recruit training:** U.S. Naval Training Station, Great Lakes, Illinois
  (the earliest letters in the collection are written from there)
- **Ship:** U.S.S. New Orleans (CA-32), a New Orleans–class heavy cruiser
- **Division:** F Division
- **At Pearl Harbor:** The New Orleans was moored at Pearl Harbor on December 7,
  1941. The first letter Gene wrote afterward (December 11, 1941) is in the
  collection, carrying the first naval-censor stamp.
- **Pacific engagements (ship's record):** Coral Sea (May 1942), Midway
  (June 1942), and the Guadalcanal campaign, including the Battle of
  Tassafaronga on the night of November 30, 1942, where a Japanese torpedo
  severed the ship's bow and killed 183 of her crew.
- **Discharge:** Honorably discharged, 1943.
- **Ship's decorations:** The U.S.S. New Orleans earned seventeen battle stars
  for World War II service.

## Open item: rate and rating

Gene's exact **rate and rating** (his job specialty and pay grade) at discharge
are not recorded in any public source consulted. They are held in his Official
Military Personnel File at the **National Personnel Records Center (NPRC),
St. Louis**, and can be requested by next of kin using **Standard Form 180
(SF-180)**:

- Request portal: archives.gov/st-louis/military-personnel
- SF-180 form: archives.gov/files/research/military/veterans/standard-form-180.pdf

Once obtained, the rating should be added here and to the biography.
