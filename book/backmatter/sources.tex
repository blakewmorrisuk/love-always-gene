\paragraph{The originals.}
The collection is roughly one hundred and ten letters, cards, and envelopes
written by Raymond Eugene Lankford to Joan Northcutt between April 1940 and
1943, with a small number of related items, including two letters written to
Gene by Navy friends after his discharge. The letters were found years after
Joan's death, folded in their original envelopes, in a box in a storage
building behind the family home in Somerset, Kentucky, by Gene and Joan's
daughter, Elizabeth ``Libby'' Lankford Morris. Their condition is generally
very good: the paper is sound and the ink legible, with only occasional
tearing and fading.

\paragraph{The family scans and transcriptions.}
The originals were first photographed by the family. An early set of
transcriptions was typed by the family before this project began; those
readings were carried forward here and checked against the images. Letters
still marked as drafts in this volume are the ones whose readings are not yet
final.

\paragraph{The 2026 rescans.}
In 2026 the originals were rephotographed page by page at higher resolution.
Those scans corrected several earlier misreadings and are the images
reproduced and linked from the online edition. Uncertain words that the new
scans still could not resolve are marked in the text.

\paragraph{Biographical research.}
A separate research document of about twenty-five pages assembled Gene's life
and the wartime history of the U.S.S. New Orleans (CA\,--\,32) from public
records and published naval histories. The biographical notes and the ship's
history in this book draw on that document; its sources are listed there.

\paragraph{Press coverage.}
The discovery of the letters was covered in February 2023 by WKYT in Lexington
(``Love Always, Gene''), by its eastern-Kentucky affiliate WYMT, and by LEX~18.

\paragraph{Conventions in the transcriptions.}
This volume preserves Gene's original spelling, punctuation, and
abbreviations. Uncertain readings of single words are marked
\textsc{[?]}. Editor's footnotes are marked with an asterisk and printed
at the foot of the page. Square brackets in the body, where they
appear, indicate words supplied by the editor to complete a torn or
illegible line. Where Gene signed twice, both signatures are
preserved.
