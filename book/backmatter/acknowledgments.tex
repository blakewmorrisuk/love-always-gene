\noindent
This book exists because my mother, Libby, opened a box she found in a
storage building behind the house and understood at once what it was. She
kept the letters safe, and she let them be read.

\par
My thanks to the family who photographed and first transcribed the originals,
and who remembered the names and the small history that the letters take for
granted. And to The National WWII Museum in New Orleans, the ship's own city,
for agreeing to be the letters' final home, so that Gene's words rest where the
New Orleans belongs.

\par
Most of all, to Gene and to Joan, who kept every one.
